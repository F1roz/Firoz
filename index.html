Research

Current work. All four manuscripts are in preparation.

Explicit 3D Geometry for AI-Generated Image Detection

Independent research, sole author. Supervisor: Raihan Uddin Ahmed. 2026

Does 3D scene geometry (depth, surfaces, lighting, perspective) help detect images made by generators a detector has never seen? I add 26 geometry features to three leading detectors, B-Free, CO-SPY and UnivFD, and test on four sets with no overlap between training and test generators.

Controls and careful statistics check that any gain comes from geometry, not from shortcuts in the data.

  • Computer vision
  • Image forensics
  • Generalization

Can a Quantum Kernel Pick Better Demonstrations? Few-Qubit Kernel Retrieval for Few-Shot LLM Classification

Elite Research Lab, first author. With A. Rahman and S. Hossain. 2026

Tests whether quantum kernels (IQP, angle and projected feature maps in PennyLane) choose better few-shot demonstrations for frozen LLMs than classical similarity does, across 4 datasets, 17 selectors and 3 LLMs (Claude Haiku, Qwen2.5-1.5B and 7B), with qubit-scaling, gate-noise and shot-noise studies.

We prove retrieval depends only on the ranking of similarities, and introduce a rank-agreement check that predicts quantum-classical differences before any LLM is called. The analysis is pre-registered, with TOST equivalence tests, cluster bootstrap CIs, Holm correction and minimum detectable effects.

  • Quantum ML
  • In-context learning
  • LLMs

Detecting Consistent Hallucinations in Large Language Models

Elite Research Lab, second author. With S. Hossain and A. Rahman. Preparing for an IEEE BigData 2026 workshop. 2026

A black-box, training-free detector for LLM errors that repeat consistently, a case sampling-based detectors such as Semantic Entropy miss. It contrasts how answers shift under targeted counter-claims versus neutral doubt.

Evaluated on 2,800+ questions from TruthfulQA, TriviaQA and SciQ with Qwen2.5-7B and Llama-3.1-8B, against seven baselines including Semantic Entropy, SelfCheckGPT, P(True) and MetaQA. Pre-registered hypotheses, AUROC with bootstrap CIs, DeLong tests with Holm correction, and a tested pipeline that regenerates every reported number from saved results.

  • LLM reliability
  • Hallucination detection
  • Evaluation

Mobile Japanese-Learning Resources for Bangladeshi Migrant Workers: An Inclusive-CALL Audit

Elite Research Lab, research intern. With Labanya Saha and S. Rahman. Target: Computer Assisted Language Learning (Taylor & Francis). 2026

An audit of free mobile Japanese-learning resources against a safety-critical workplace phrase inventory built from government and institutional sources, using an inclusive-design coding scheme (offline access, audio-first, low literacy, Bangla support) with double-coding and inter-rater reliability. The findings feed requirements for an offline learning module.

  • HCI
  • Language learning
  • Inclusive design
